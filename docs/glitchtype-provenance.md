## Fonts and icons

The supplied official VT323 Regular TTF is bundled unmodified under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1. The SHA-256 is
`cf4de751ada78ceac033dbe16a687742939995b77bc2a052ae17a4957958594d`.
Its notice is in `src/assets/fonts/OFL-VT323.txt` and ships under
`licenses/OFL-VT323.txt`. [Google Fonts source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/vt323/VT323-Regular.ttf).

VT323 is used for the portal title, with Space Grotesk and the system sans-serif
stack as glyph fallbacks, including for Ukrainian. Body, metrics and labels use
the existing local sans-serif and monospace families with system fallbacks.
The portal retains its existing icons. The JetBrains Mono font, Phosphor icons,
preview artwork and reference stylesheet are not bundled.
